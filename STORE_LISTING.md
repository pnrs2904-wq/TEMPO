## Full Description
Most focus timers assume every session should be the same length. Tempo doesn't.

Before you start, tell Tempo how you're feeling — from very low to very high energy. It recommends a focus duration built for that state, not a generic 25-minute default.

### It learns, but it doesn't guess.
Tempo tracks how your sessions actually go — whether you finish them, how focused you felt, how difficult the material was. Over time, it adjusts your recommended duration up or down, in small, careful steps. It never pushes you longer when recent sessions were hard or distracted — it holds steady or scales back instead.

### It tells you when it disagrees with you.
If you say a session felt "too short" but your recent focus quality suggests otherwise, Tempo doesn't just silently override you — it tells you what it noticed and why. No black-box adjustments.

### It gets more specific the more you use it.
Once you've logged enough sessions in a subject, Tempo learns separate patterns per subject — because your ideal Math session and your ideal Reading session probably aren't the same length.

### Built for real study, not just productivity theater.
- Session types for Study, Revision, Practice Questions, and Mock Tests
- Daily and weekly goal tracking, with exam countdown
- Streaks that only count sessions you actually finish
- Full session history with focus quality, difficulty, and notes
- Export your data anytime as JSON or CSV — it's yours
- Optional AI-generated weekly insights from your own session notes
- Fully functional offline — no account, no network dependency for the core experience

Tempo isn't trying to gamify focus. It's trying to get the number right, and be honest with you about how it got there.
